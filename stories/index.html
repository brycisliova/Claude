Courage, in public

Stories from queer India

People who lived openly when it was dangerous, so the rest of us could breathe a little easier. Their stories are retold here in brief, with links to read more.

Trailblazers

AR

Ashok Row Kavi

Journalist, founder of Humsafar Trust

Spoke openly about being gay in Indian media in the 1980s, when almost no one did. Founded Bombay Dost, India's first gay magazine, and the Humsafar Trust, which still runs helplines and clinics.

Read more
LN

Laxmi Narayan Tripathi

Trans rights activist

A key voice in the NALSA case that recognised transgender people in 2014. Her memoir is Me Hijra, Me Laxmi. She heads the Kinnar Akhada and has represented Asia-Pacific in UN HIV/AIDS forums.

Read more
MS

Manvendra Singh Gohil

Prince of Rajpipla

Came out in 2006, the first Indian royal to do so, and faced public rejection from his family. Founded the Lakshya Trust for HIV awareness and queer support.

Read more
NS

Navtej Singh Johar

Bharatanatyam dancer

Lead petitioner in the 2018 case that struck down Section 377. His name is now on the judgment that set millions free.

Read more
KS

Keshav Suri

Hotelier, 377 petitioner

Another petitioner in the 2018 case. Married his partner Cyril Feuillebois. His mother and sisters supported him from the start, and his foundation funds scholarships and mental health support for queer people.

Read more
RD

Ritu Dalmia

Chef, 377 petitioner

Came out to her parents at 23 and later joined the Supreme Court petition alongside other prominent Indians.

Read more
DC

Dutee Chand

Olympic sprinter

In 2019 became India's first openly gay elite athlete, speaking about her relationship despite opposition at home.

Read more
GB

Grace Banu

Dalit trans activist, engineer

The first transgender person admitted to an engineering college in Tamil Nadu. Campaigns for reservations and dignity for trans people.

Read more
AG

Anjali Gopalan

Founder, Naz Foundation

Filed the 2001 petition against Section 377 that began India's legal journey to equality.

Read more
SK

Saurabh Kirpal

Senior advocate

In 2021 the Supreme Court collegium recommended him as a Delhi High Court judge, the first openly gay person recommended for the role in India.

Read more
JM

Joyita Mondal

Judge, activist

Became one of India's first transgender judges when appointed to a Lok Adalat in West Bengal in 2017, after years of rejection and homelessness.

Read more
PP

Padmini Prakash

News anchor

Became India's first transgender television news anchor in 2014 in Tamil Nadu.

Read more
