# Who We Are

*Not a choice. Not an illness. Just who we are.*

A calm, research-backed website for understanding LGBTQIA+ lives: the science, the history, queer India, therapies, sound, games, and a place to be heard. No tracking, no cookies, no ads.

Address: <https://shiningforever.in>

## What is on the site

- **Understand:** LGBTQIA+ explained, the science, a research library, a timeline, and myths and facts.
- **Stories:** trailblazers, the community wall, voices and videos, films and series, India and the world in data, and a news page refreshed every night.
- **Care:** helplines and guides, resources for LGBTQIA+ students who want to study abroad, therapies, a sound studio, and calm games.
- **Helper chat:** a small chat on every page that finds pages, answers common questions and shows helplines.
- **Games:** party games and quizzes to play with friends, plus calm games to play alone.
- **About:** about the builder, how to get in touch, privacy and safety, sources and photo credits.

## Games

Play alone, or make a room and play with friends on their own phones. No sign-up. The host can change the game or its settings inside the same room, and everyone stays in.

- **Truth or Dare:** from mild to wild, with an 18+ level, plus Never have I ever and Spill the tea.
- **Name that tune:** hear a short clip and type the song. Hindi games use only Hindi and Punjabi songs. Every song is graded for how many people know it, and a game plays them from the best known to the least known.
- **Song antakshari:** sing a line, then the next player must start with your last sound. Hindi songs can be typed in Hinglish or Hindi, and spelling never matters.
- **Word antakshari:** a word chain in English or Hinglish where each word starts with the last letter of the one before.
- **Atlas:** name places in a chain: cities, villages, states, countries, continents, oceans and deserts.
- **Where in the world?:** drop a pin on a map to find where a photo was taken.
- **Trivia night** and the **Pride pop quiz:** fresh questions every game. The pop quiz is about music, films, Bollywood, drag and the stars everyone loves, and every question was fact-checked.
- **Adda night:** party games for friends. After a Hot take there is time to argue it out, and the host can pause, add time or move on.
- **Calm games:** breathing, lanterns, sand gardens, puzzles and more.

## Fair play

The bots in Atlas and Word antakshari pick places and words the way a person would. They never choose an answer to leave you with a hard letter. Twists are optional, announced ahead and the same for everyone. If a word ends in a very rare letter, the next one starts with the letter before it, for you and the bot alike.

## Behind the scenes

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. A scheduled workflow (`.github/workflows/news.yml`) gathers the news every night at about 2:20 AM India time. The helper chat works on its own in the browser, and gains open conversation when the small Cloudflare Worker in `backend/` is deployed (setup is in `backend/README.md`).

## Privacy

The site runs in your browser and your scores stay on your device. See the Privacy and safety page for exactly what is and is not shared.
