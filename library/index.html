26 papers, honestly described

The research library

Every key paper, what it found, and its limitations. Filter by question or search by author, journal or topic. Every title links to the original.

Papers

1948

Sexual Behavior in the Human Male

Kinsey, Pomeroy & Martin · Book, W.B. Saunders

Interviews with thousands of Americans showed same-sex attraction was far more common than assumed, and that sexuality varies along a spectrum.

Not a choice

Limits: Samples were not random, so exact percentages are unreliable. Its lasting contribution was showing diversity, not precise rates.

1957

The adjustment of the male overt homosexual

Evelyn Hooker · Journal of Projective Techniques

Expert clinicians shown blind personality tests from 30 gay and 30 straight men couldn't tell them apart. Gay men were as psychologically healthy.

Not an illness

Limits: Small sample of non-patient men from one city, but it was the first rigorous challenge to the illness model.

1991

A genetic study of male sexual orientation

Bailey & Pillard · Archives of General Psychiatry

Identical twins of gay men were much more likely to be gay than fraternal twins or adopted brothers, pointing to a genetic influence.

Not a choice

Limits: Volunteers were recruited through ads, which likely inflated concordance. Later population studies found smaller but still real genetic effects.

1991

A difference in hypothalamic structure between heterosexual and homosexual men

Simon LeVay · Science

A small brain region (INAH3) was on average smaller in gay men than in straight men.

Not a choice

Limits: Only 41 post-mortem brains, many affected by AIDS. Important as a first clue; not proof on its own.

1993

A linkage between DNA markers on the X chromosome and male sexual orientation

Hamer et al. · Science

Reported a link between a region called Xq28 and male homosexuality in families with several gay brothers.

Not a choice

Limits: Replication was mixed. A larger 2015 study (Sanders et al.) supported it, but the 2019 GWAS did not find it. Treat as unsettled.

1996

Homosexuality in men and number of older brothers

Blanchard & Bogaert · American Journal of Psychiatry

Each older brother increased the odds of a man being gay. This fraternal birth order effect is one of the most replicated findings in the field.

Not a choice

Limits: Explains only a portion of gay men. It is a population pattern, not a rule for individuals.

2003

Prejudice, social stress, and mental health in LGB populations

Ilan Meyer · Psychological Bulletin

Introduced the minority stress model: higher rates of mental health problems among LGB people are explained by stigma and discrimination, not orientation itself.

Not an illnessMental health

Limits: A review and theoretical model; many later studies have since tested and supported it.

2006

Biological versus nonbiological older brothers and men's sexual orientation

Anthony Bogaert · PNAS

Only older brothers born to the same mother mattered, even if raised apart. Stepbrothers or adopted brothers raised together had no effect. Points to the womb, not upbringing.

Not a choice

Limits: Observational data; the biological mechanism was proposed later.

2008

PET and MRI show differences in cerebral asymmetry and functional connectivity between homo- and heterosexual subjects

Savic & Lindström · PNAS

Brain symmetry patterns of gay men resembled straight women's, and lesbians' resembled straight men's, in areas unlikely to be shaped by learning.

Not a choice

Limits: 90 participants. Brain differences can't by themselves say what caused them.

2009

Family rejection as a predictor of negative health outcomes in LGB young adults

Ryan et al. · Pediatrics

Young people facing high family rejection were about 8.4 times more likely to report suicide attempts than those facing little or none.

Mental healthFamily

Limits: Cross-sectional US data, so it shows strong association rather than proven cause.

2009

Appropriate therapeutic responses to sexual orientation

American Psychological Association Task Force · APA report

A systematic review found no reliable evidence that efforts to change sexual orientation work, and evidence of harm.

Change efforts

Limits: Most change-effort studies it reviewed were themselves low quality, which is part of its conclusion.

2010

Genetic and environmental effects on same-sex sexual behavior: a population study of twins in Sweden

Långström et al. · Archives of Sexual Behavior

About 3,800 twin pairs. Genes explained roughly a third of variation in men and a fifth in women; shared family environment explained almost none.

Not a choice

Limits: Measures behaviour rather than identity, and same-sex behaviour was rare, so estimates have wide uncertainty.

2010

US National Longitudinal Lesbian Family Study: psychological adjustment of 17-year-old adolescents

Gartrell & Bos · Pediatrics

Teenagers raised by lesbian mothers since birth were rated higher in social and academic competence and lower in behaviour problems than a normative sample.

Family

Limits: Volunteer families, mostly well-off and white, so not representative.

2011

The social environment and suicide attempts in LGB youth

Mark Hatzenbuehler · Pediatrics

LGB youth in more supportive social environments had a significantly lower risk of suicide attempts.

Mental health

Limits: One US state's data; county-level environment measures.

2015

Genome-wide scan demonstrates significant linkage for male sexual orientation

Sanders et al. · Psychological Medicine

In 409 pairs of gay brothers, found linkage to regions on chromosome 8 and Xq28.

Not a choice

Limits: Linkage studies are coarse; the 2019 GWAS found different specific loci.

2015

LGB-affirmative cognitive-behavioral therapy for young adult gay and bisexual men

Pachankis et al. · Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology

A therapy targeting minority stress (ESTEEM) reduced depression and alcohol use.

Mental health

Limits: Small pilot trial of 63 men; larger trials have followed.

2016

Sexual orientation, controversy, and science

Bailey et al. · Psychological Science in the Public Interest

A major review concluding there is considerably more evidence for non-social causes of sexual orientation than social ones, and no good evidence it can be changed by therapy or upbringing.

Not a choiceChange efforts

Limits: A review, so it depends on the studies it summarises, which it critiques openly.

2017

Difference-in-differences analysis of state same-sex marriage policies and adolescent suicide attempts

Raifman et al. · JAMA Pediatrics

After US states legalised same-sex marriage, high-school suicide attempts fell by about 7%, with larger drops among sexual minority students.

Mental health

Limits: Strong design for observational data, though it can't rule out every other change.

2017

Music therapy for depression

Aalbers et al. · Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews

Music therapy added to usual care probably reduces depressive symptoms and anxiety in the short term.

Mental health

Limits: Low to moderate certainty; studies varied in quality.

2018

Male homosexuality and maternal immune responsivity to the Y-linked protein NLGN4Y

Bogaert et al. · PNAS

Mothers of gay sons, especially those with older brothers, had higher antibody levels to a male-specific brain protein, a possible mechanism for the older-brother effect.

Not a choice

Limits: Small sample; needs replication.

2019

Large-scale GWAS reveals insights into the genetic architecture of same-sex sexual behavior

Ganna et al. · Science

477,522 people. No single gay gene; many variants with small effects. Common variants together explained 8 to 25% of variation.

Not a choice

Limits: Measured ever having a same-sex partner, mostly in people of European ancestry. Authors warned against using it to predict anyone's orientation.

2019

An alternative hypothesis for the evolution of same-sex sexual behaviour in animals

Monk et al. · Nature Ecology & Evolution

Proposes same-sex behaviour may have been part of animal life from early in evolution, rather than an anomaly needing special explanation.

Nature

Limits: A theoretical paper that calls for more data.

2020

Parent-initiated sexual orientation change efforts with LGBT adolescents

Ryan et al. · Journal of Homosexuality

Young adults whose parents tried to change their orientation had higher depression and suicide attempt rates and lower education and income.

Change effortsFamily

Limits: Retrospective self-reports from one US sample.

2020

Association between recalled exposure to gender identity conversion efforts and psychological distress and suicide attempts

Turban et al. · JAMA Psychiatry

Trans adults exposed to conversion efforts had higher odds of lifetime suicide attempts, especially if exposed before age 10.

Change effortsMental health

Limits: Cross-sectional survey data.

2020

Sexual orientation change efforts, adverse childhood experiences, and suicide ideation and attempt

Blosnich et al. · American Journal of Public Health

Sexual minority adults who went through change efforts had nearly twice the odds of suicidal thoughts and higher odds of attempts, even after accounting for childhood adversity.

Change effortsMental health

Limits: Observational; US national sample.

2025

LGBTQ+ suicides in Indian news media, 2001–2022

Dialogues in Health · Dialogues in Health

Analysis of 164 Indian news reports: family non-acceptance and forced separation were the most common narratives; joint couple suicides were frequent.

Mental healthFamily

Limits: Based on news coverage, so it reflects what gets reported, not all cases.

How to read this library. One study never settles a question. Small or early studies (like LeVay 1991 or Hamer 1993) were important but had limits, and some findings didn't fully replicate. The conclusions on this site rest on what many independent studies, across countries and decades, keep finding.
