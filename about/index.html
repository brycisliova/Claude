About the builder

Reiner Endrios

Not my real name. A name for the part of me that stayed.

I said it out loud for the first time in college,
in a room I thought was safe.

The laughter came quickly.
Not cruel, they said. Just a joke.
But jokes don't follow you home.

Some friends drifted. Some stayed and never asked again,
which is its own kind of leaving.

I learned the shape of loneliness:
a full hostel corridor,
and a door I kept closed anyway.

I learned to laugh along. I learned to go quiet.
I learned how heavy it is to edit yourself
before every sentence.

And still, some mornings, the light comes in.
A song. A stranger's kindness. A line in a paper
that says you were never broken.

I'm still trying.
Not finished, not fixed, not fearless.
Just here.

I built this place for the version of me
who sat alone in that room.
If that's you right now: stay.
It gets less lonely. I promise to keep building until it does.

Reiner
