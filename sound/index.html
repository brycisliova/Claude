Sound studio

Every sound here is generated live in your browser, so there's nothing to download. Mix rain, ocean and wind, strike a singing bowl, or let calm music compose itself. Headphones make it better.

Nature mixer

Instruments

Singing bowl

A long, shimmering tone. Strike it, breathe out slowly, and listen until it fades.

Ambient music

Gentle notes that compose themselves, never repeating.

Binaural beats

Two slightly different tones, one in each ear. Many people find them relaxing, though scientific evidence for specific effects is mixed. Headphones required.

Sleep timer

Everything fades out gently.

Song therapy

Songs

Music affects mood in measurable ways. A Cochrane review (Aalbers et al., 2017) found music therapy added to usual care probably reduces depressive symptoms in the short term. Here are songs many queer people say helped them feel less alone. Press play to listen here.
