Want to help this grow, collaborate, translate, volunteer, or just say hello? Write to shiningforevr@gmail.com or use the forms below.

Anonymous message

Share an experience, a hope, a struggle, or kind words for someone reading. No name or email is asked for. Messages are read and approved before anything appears on the community wall.

Anonymous sending is being switched on. Until it is, your message can't be delivered from this page, and emailing isn't anonymous.

Please keep it kind; hateful or harmful messages are never posted. Avoid details that could identify you or someone else: like any website, the service that delivers a message handles it technically.
